TrackpadSwipe — macOS 15 or later

1. Quit any older TrackpadSwipe/TrackpadType copy.
2. Drag TrackpadSwipe.app to Applications, then eject this disk image.
3. Open TrackpadSwipe from Applications.
4. In System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility, add the installed
   TrackpadSwipe.app and enable it. Keep the app in the same location for updates.
5. Click into an editable text field. Rest three fingers on the trackpad and
   physically click to open the keyboard. Touching alone does not activate it.
6. Click a starting letter, trace the word, then click again to enter it.
   Words get a leading space only when needed; no automatic trailing space.
   Fn opens numbers/punctuation; ABC returns to letters. Click symbols to enter.
   Swipe contractions through their letters; choose the apostrophized suggestion.
   The on-screen delete key removes a word or selected text.
7. Press Esc or three-finger click again to close and release capture.

Choose Settings from the keyboard menu-bar icon for size, position, pointer speed,
suggestion fonts, cursor appearance, and alternative triggers.

A package marked UNNOTARIZED-preview is a local test build, not a notarized public
release. See the online guide for first-launch behavior and troubleshooting.

Known limits: all pointing devices are captured during a stroke; private trackpad
APIs may conflict with system gestures; editor compatibility varies. Codex and Telegram use
process-targeted typing with a remaining focus-timing limitation. Intel binaries
are included but have not been tested on Intel hardware.

the app to Trash. No privileged installer, service, or login item is installed.
