## Important
- **Successful AX writes can be reported as failures and restore a stale selection.** The context reader at `source/native/main.swift:274–282` falls back to AXValue when parameterized range reads are unavailable; verification at `source/native/main.swift:297–302` does not. A selected-text setter can therefore change the editor successfully while verification reports failure. The transaction then restores the original selection at `source/native/main.swift:213`: a collapsed caret moves before the inserted word, while a formerly nonempty selection can cover the replacement and following text. Subsequent input can insert in the wrong position or overwrite unintended content, and correction is unavailable. Use one bounded UTF-16 range reader for context and verification, and distinguish rejected writes from uncertain postconditions after mutation. Do not restore the pre-mutation selection solely because read-back failed; reconcile the actual destination or stop edits until it is recaptured. Add an adapter-level regression where parameterized reads are unsupported, AXValue reflects the successful mutation, and two words append successfully. Existing transaction fakes bypass this production read-back path. **Effort:** moderate. **Personas:** Hypercritical, Data-Integrity, Test.

## Minor
- **The focus-gates test bypasses the production eligibility predicate.** Tests at `source/native/main.swift:1108–1116` inject constant true/false validators into the emitter. They do not exercise the predicate at `source/native/main.swift:261`, so removing its original-field or selection comparison would leave the tests green and permit correction input into a different field or changed selection. Extract the predicate behind injected app, field, and range reads; independently assert zero emissions for changed PID, changed field, changed selection, and cancellation during validation. Keep the emitter fake. This deterministic validation gap is distinct from the asynchronous focus race below. **Effort:** moderate. **Persona:** Test.

## Noted
- **Process-targeted input retains a documented focus race.** `source/native/main.swift:258–264` validates the original field, selection, foreground PID, and cancellation immediately before posting, but delivery addresses the process rather than the AX element. A later focus change inside Codex can redirect committed text or Backspace to another control. The limitation is disclosed in `source/docs/PRIVACY.md:11` and release-readiness documentation; no reproducible exploit or external attacker path was established. Field-addressed delivery would remove the limitation; disabling the fallback is an alternative if strict field binding is required. **Effort:** significant for an equivalent compatible field-addressed route; trivial to disable the fallback. **Personas:** Adversarial; also observed by Data-Integrity and Thousand-Foot.
